About Oasis

The first school your child will remember

Strong roots first, so little ones can fly with confidence.

Oasis Preschool Academy is a neighbourhood preschool in Kurmannapalem, close to Gajuwaka and Duvvada. We care for children in their earliest school years, when habits, curiosity and confidence take shape. Every day mixes play, stories, songs, early letters and numbers, art and outdoor time.

Learning through play

Hands-on activity kits, sensory play, rhymes and stories at every level.

Personal attention

Small classes so teachers know each child, their friends and their pace.

Ready for Class 1

Steady steps in reading, writing and numbers from Playgroup through UKG.

Festivals and events

Independence Day, festivals and term activities celebrated together.

Classes

Four steps, one growing journey

Each class builds on the last, from first play and making friends to reading simple words and counting with confidence.

1First step

Playgroup

1½ to 3 years

  • A gentle first time away from home
  • Free play, music and movement
  • Sand, water, clay and sensory play
  • Talking, listening and simple rhymes
2Second step

Nursery

3 to 4 years

  • Settling in, routines and making friends
  • Rhymes, stories and picture talk
  • Colours, shapes and sensory play
  • Early counting and fine-motor activities
3Third step

LKG

4 to 5 years

  • Letters, phonics and first sounds
  • Numbers and simple counting games
  • Drawing, colouring and craft
  • Speaking, sharing and show-and-tell
4Fourth step

UKG

5 to 6 years

  • Reading simple words and sentences
  • Writing practice and number work
  • Homework that builds good habits
  • Report cards and Class 1 readiness

Admissions

Joining Oasis in four steps

  1. Get in touch

    Call, WhatsApp or send the enquiry form below. We'll suggest a time to visit.

  2. Visit the school

    Bring your child, see the classrooms and meet the teachers.

  3. Fill the admission form

    Your child's name and date of birth, parents' names, phone numbers and address, and whether you need the van.

  4. Confirm the seat

    Pay the admission fee. You'll get your parent app login before the first day.
