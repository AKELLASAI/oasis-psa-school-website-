Admissions 2026–27

Join Oasis Preschool Academy in Kurmannapalem, Visakhapatnam for Playgroup, Nursery, LKG or UKG. Here's everything you need to know before you visit.

Joining Oasis in four steps

  1. Get in touch. Call, WhatsApp or send the enquiry form. We'll suggest a time to visit.
  2. Visit the school. Bring your child, see the classrooms and meet the teachers.
  3. Fill the admission form. Your child's name and date of birth, parents' names, phone numbers and address, and whether you need the van.
  4. Confirm the seat. Pay the admission fee. You'll get your parent app login before the first day.

Classes and ages

Playgroup

1½ to 3 years

A gentle first time away from home with free play, music, rhymes and sensory play.

Nursery

3 to 4 years

Routines, friends, stories, colours, shapes and early counting.

LKG

4 to 5 years

Letters, phonics, first sounds, numbers, drawing and craft.

UKG

5 to 6 years

Reading simple words, writing practice, number work and Class 1 readiness.

Fees

Fees depend on the class and on whether your child uses the van. The fee types are:

  • Admission fee (one time)
  • Tuition fee (monthly)
  • School van fee (monthly, if used)
  • Books and uniform (yearly)
  • Activity fee (per term)

Fees can be paid by UPI, and you get a receipt once the office confirms payment. Please ask the office for the exact amounts.
